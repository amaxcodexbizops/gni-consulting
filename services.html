Services Designed to

Service 01

Credit Repair & Dispute Services

We review your full credit report across all three bureaus, identify inaccurate, outdated, or unverifiable negative items, and dispute them on your behalf — so your score reflects your real financial picture.

Best for: Anyone with collections, late payments, charge-offs, or errors holding their score back
  • 3-bureau credit report analysis
  • Custom dispute letters to Equifax, Experian & TransUnion
  • Creditor goodwill intervention letters
  • Ongoing progress monitoring & updates
  • Credit-building strategy recommendations

Service 02

Business Credit & Funding Assistance

Build a fundable business profile from the ground up — or repair an existing one. We help you establish business credit that's separate from your personal score and position you to access real capital.

Best for: Entrepreneurs, small business owners, and anyone ready to access business funding
  • Business entity & EIN setup guidance
  • D&B, Experian Business & Equifax Business profile setup
  • Net-30 vendor account strategy
  • Business credit line & loan readiness review
  • Funding source matching
Get Started

Service 03

Financial Coaching

One-on-one guidance to help you understand how credit works, develop healthy financial habits, and build the confidence to make decisions that serve your long-term goals — not just today's emergency.

Best for: Clients who want to understand the "why" behind their credit and take lasting control of their finances
  • Credit score education & goal mapping
  • Personalized action plan
  • Debt payoff strategy (avalanche vs. snowball)
  • Credit utilization & limit optimization
  • Monthly accountability check-ins

Service 04

Budgeting & Financial Planning

Credit repair doesn't work in a vacuum. We help you build a realistic budget, identify where money is leaking, and create a financial plan that supports both your credit goals and your life.

Best for: Clients who feel like they're spinning their wheels financially and want a clear, workable plan
  • Income & expense audit
  • Monthly zero-based budget build
  • Savings goal framework
  • Emergency fund strategy
  • Long-term financial milestone planning
